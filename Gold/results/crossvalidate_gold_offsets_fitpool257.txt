=============================================================

contributes to the offset that judges it. A landmark is adopted only if a majority
of folds found a bias of at least 2.0 ms with a bootstrap interval clear of zero,
and the pooled out of sample absolute error did not get significantly worse.

The offsets written out are the ones fitted on the full reviewed half, since that is
the better estimate of each number. What is cross validated is the decision to adopt
them at all, and the fold to fold spread reported below is the evidence that the
number does not depend on which records happened to be in the fit.

LIMITATION. These records are not a fresh sample. They were reviewed knowing which
landmarks the estimator was likely to get wrong, so the benefit reported here is an
upper bound. A genuinely untouched half would be stronger evidence and the design
still holds 400 such records in gold_worklist_test_seeded.csv if there is ever time.
